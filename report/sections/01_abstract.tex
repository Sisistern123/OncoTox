\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Relapse is driven by rare surviving subpopulations of tumour cells, yet drug response is measured on
bulk populations, and no large-scale single-cell drug screen exists. This report asks three questions:
whether anti-cancer drug response can be predicted from single cells with bulk labels at all; whether
scGPT embeddings are a viable representation against principal component analysis (PCA) at matched
width; and whether a model trained on single cells learns cellular heterogeneity implicitly. On the
intersection of the SCP542 atlas and CTRPv2 as reprocessed by DrEval (\NLines{} cell lines), one
response value per cell line and compound was broadcast onto every cell of that line, so the effective
sample size was about \NLinesCV{} lines rather than \NCells{} cells. A per-cell multi-task regressor was
trained on \NPanel{} compounds chosen by regulatory approval and a published resistance determinant, and
scored by the mean within-compound Spearman correlation under five-fold leave-cell-line-out
cross-validation over three seeds.

The highest-scoring configuration reached \SpBest{}, ranging from \SpDrugLo{} to \SpDrugHi{} across
compounds and tracking the share of flat dose--response curves. A ridge regression on cell-line means
reached \RidgePca{}, and a per-compound random forest matched the model on the DrEval benchmark; over all
\NDrugsModelled{} compounds the score fell to \SpAllDrugs{}, at most \NullGainMax{} above a per-compound
constant, and about a fifth of the ranking was general cell-line fragility. PCA led scGPT in all six
loss and weighting configurations, and before any training it carried more between-line structure
(between-to-within variance ratio \BtwRatioPca{} against \BtwRatioScgpt{}). The label thus sits at the
granularity a foundation-model embedding is built to suppress, a statement about evaluation design
rather than about scGPT. Whether heterogeneity is learned remains open: a multiple-instance model
separated a line's cells but not two lines known to differ. The binding constraint is the supply and
quality of labels, not model capacity.
\end{abstract}
