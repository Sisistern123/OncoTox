\chapter{Implementation details}
\label{app:implementation}

This appendix holds the implementation details behind three steps of Chapter~\ref{sec:methods}. They
are needed to reproduce the analysis but not to follow its results.

\section{Compound identifier resolution}
\label{app:ids}

Matching the approval list to the screen was not done on names, because approval lists use
international nonproprietary names, often of salts, while screens use development codes and free bases.
In addition, CTRPv2's compound annotation \cite{ctrpv2} and DrEval's response file \cite{dreval} name
\NameMismatch{} of the \NDrugs{} compounds differently, \NameMismatchFda{} of them approved or clinical. Four keys were therefore
tried and the successful one recorded per compound: both name spaces, the PubChem compound identifier,
and PubChem's parent-compound relation \cite{pubchem}, which links a salt to the dissolved free base and
alone accounted for \NParentMatch{} matches, among them imatinib and doxorubicin. The two internal files were joined
on the screen's own compound identifier, which matched all \NDrugs{}. Cisplatin required one explicit
correction: it is recorded under a non-standard name with a structure identifier for elemental
platinum, and was resolved on the screen's recorded structure and vendor entry.

\section{Gene symbols and the scGPT vocabulary}
\label{app:hgnc}

scGPT matches genes to its vocabulary by symbol, and the expression matrix carries an older annotation of
the HUGO Gene Nomenclature Committee (HGNC) than the vocabulary \cite{hgnc}. Renamed genes would therefore
have been discarded although the vocabulary holds them under their current name: \NRescuedHvg{} genes of
the \NHvg-gene variant and \NRescuedAll{} of the full-transcriptome variant, the latter carrying
\PctRescuedAll\,\% of each cell's transcriptome, and because of the per-cell binning every remaining gene
would have shifted bin as well. The conversion step therefore recorded each gene's current HGNC symbol,
and the embedding step used it where the distributed symbol is not in the vocabulary, which raised the
\NHvg-gene variant from \NVocabDirect{} to \NVocab{} embedded genes.

\section{Confound covariates and cached fits}
\label{app:confounds}

The adjusted $R^2$ was used because the unadjusted one is biased upward by the number of covariates by an
amount that shrinks with the number of cells, so one bar would differ between a line of \CellsMin{} cells and one
of \CellsMax{}. On simulated predictions unrelated to the covariates the unadjusted value read about 19\,\%
and the adjusted about $-1\,\%$. Depth and mitochondrial fraction cannot be computed from the CPM matrix,
from which library size has been divided out and in which only four of 13 mitochondrial genes are
HVGs, so both were computed from the study's unnormalised UMI matrix over all genes: a total over HVGs
alone is depth multiplied by a biological share, and a confounder carrying the signal under test could
veto a true positive \cite{scp542,luecken2019}.
The per-fold standardisation and PCA fits of Section~\ref{sec:representations} were cached per run,
keyed by the reproducible fold assignment, in single precision like the decomposition over all CV lines used for the input ceiling, which
was kept for description only.

\section{Quality control of the atlas}
\label{app:qc}

SCP542 is distributed after the source study's own quality control \cite{scp542}. Cells had been
profiled in \NPools{} multiplexed pools on the 10x Chromium
platform \cite{zheng2017}, at about \SrcCellsPerLine{} cells per line, processed with Cell Ranger 3.0.1, and assigned
to lines by the consensus of a genotype-based and an expression-based method. Cells were retained when
their number of detected genes lay between \QcGenesLo{} and \QcGenesHi{} --- the floor removes droplets that
captured almost no RNA, the ceiling droplets holding two cells --- and lines with fewer than \QcMinCells{} cells
were removed, leaving an average of \SrcUmiMean{} UMIs and \SrcGenesMean{} detected
genes per cell across \NGenes{} genes \cite{scp542,luecken2019}. A second, generic filter was not
applied, because the decisive doublet call rests on genotype, which the distributed matrix does not
carry. An expression-based pass would remove genuine cells at the margins without recovering that
signal \cite{scp542,luecken2019}. Two consequences were accepted: any statement about heterogeneity
concerns cells inside that window, which excludes states of low transcript content such as quiescent
cells near its floor; and the \NLines{} labelled lines, not the \NCtrpLines{} lines the screen covers,
bound the sample \cite{scp542,dreval}. The metadata also carry per-cell scores for the 12 recurrent
heterogeneity programs of the source study. The scores for EMT-II and EMT-III are identical in all
\NCells{} cells of the distributed file, although the source study describes two distinct programs, so
only eleven distinct programs were available \cite{scp542,scp542_portal}.

\section{Response labels}
\label{app:labels}

The order of operations distinguishes DrEval's refitted AUC from CTRPv2's published AUC: CTRPv2
averaged its duplicate measurements during normalisation \cite{ctrpv2}, as is standard practice, whereas
DrEval normalises each replicate to its own drug-free control and fits one curve across all replicates,
so that disagreement between replicates enters the fit and its quality control instead of being averaged
away \cite{dreval,bayer2023curvecurator}. Pairs screened in more than one CTRPv2 experiment arrive as one
curve fitted across their replicates. The pipeline dropped the exact duplicates this produced and raised
an error on any pair that was not identical.
% (\code{scripts/preprocessing/ctrp\_to\_h5ad.py})

\section{scGPT input}
\label{app:scgpt-input}

Within scGPT's cell-embedding routine the only operation applied to expression was a binning of each
cell's non-zero values at quantiles computed on that cell alone, into \CfgBins{} bin values of which one
is reserved for zero, and the model read a sequence of at most \CfgMaxGenes{} tokens: the cell token and
the cell's non-zero vocabulary genes, drawn at random when there are more \cite{scgpt,scgpt_code}. scGPT
therefore received the same CPM matrix as PCA without logarithm or standardisation. This asymmetry is
deliberate: binning is a rank transform within a cell, so any monotone per-cell transformation ---
library-size division, logarithm, or both --- leaves every bin unchanged, and applying the PCA
preprocessing to both arms would alter nothing scGPT sees while disguising that the two consume
different information \cite{scgpt}. It also means the choice of source matrix is open only for PCA:
substituting the pre-quality-control UMI counts would change gene selection and the components but not
the scGPT input.

Weight decay was zero because no sourced value for AdamW existed. A coefficient chosen for Adam, which
couples decay into the adaptive gradient, means something different under AdamW, which applies it to the
parameters directly \cite{loshchilov2019adamw}. Biases and normalisation
parameters were nonetheless excluded from decay, following the convention of the \code{transformers}
library's trainer \cite{wolf2020transformers}, so that the question can later be settled by one coefficient.
Because the AUC lies near one rather than zero and its mean differs per compound, from \PanelMeanLo{} to
\PanelMeanHi{}, each head's bias was initialised at its compound's mean over the fold's fitting lines, so
that the first epochs are not spent shifting predictions to that level and early stopping cannot select a
checkpoint before any between-line structure is fitted \cite{lin2017focal}.
Early stopping restored the checkpoint of lowest error on the inner hold-out. The per-compound
means and head initialisation used the remaining fitting lines. This three-way split follows the DrEval
benchmark \cite{dreval}. The \CfgHoldoutPct\,\% is arbitrary and was preferred to reusing a whole fold, which would
have cost \CfgFoldPct\,\% of the training lines.

\section{Controls}
\label{app:controls}

The ridge regression on cell-line means was fitted on each fold's fitting lines, the training lines
less those held out for early stopping, so that it learns from the same lines as the MLP. It chose its
penalty from \CfgGridN{} log-spaced values between $10^{-4}$ and $10^{6}$ by efficient leave-one-out
error \cite{hastie2009,pedregosa2011}. Features entered unscaled, since a log-spaced grid is invariant
to feature scale up to a shift. Of \RidgeFitsPerRep{} fits per representation, \RidgeFloorPca{} PCA fits
chose the lowest penalty of the grid and \RidgeCeilPca{} PCA and \RidgeCeilScgpt{} scGPT fits the highest, so
for these the grid rather than the data set the penalty. A fit at the highest penalty predicts close to
the compound mean.
% (\code{scripts/evaluation/ridge\_grid\_refit.py}, \code{--edges} for \code{panel/ridge\_alpha\_per\_fit.csv}.
% Rewritten 21.10.2026: until then this described the 17-value grid on the whole training side.)
DrEval's normalised metric subtracts a fitted overall mean, cell-line effect and compound effect from
truth and prediction \cite{dreval}, of which under LCO only the compound effect can be estimated
(Section~\ref{sec:evaluation}).
On DrEval's LCO protocol the MLP was run in the default configuration, the trunk with the unweighted
MAE objective. It was trained on the cells of each fold's training lines, stopped early on DrEval's
validation lines and scored on its test lines, once for each of seeds 42--44, and its score is the mean
over seeds. The PCA was refitted per fold on the training lines' cells, and the random forest received
the line means of the same projection. DrEval's forest sets no random seed, so it was seeded with 42.

\section{Density weight}
\label{app:density}

The density $\hat p_j$ of Equation~\ref{eq:density} is a Gaussian kernel density estimate with Scott's
bandwidth \cite{scott1992}, fitted on one label per fitting line of the fold. It was fitted per compound because the metric is
within-compound, and on one label per line rather than per cell because every cell carries its line's
label: a line with \CellsMax{} cells would otherwise bend the density while one with \CellsMin{} barely
registered. The cap $c = \CfgCap$ is a safety limit against a few extreme lines driving a fit over about
a hundred points, the fitting lines of one fold. It is arbitrary and was held fixed, since sweeping it with $\alpha$ would confound
the two. The exponent was not justified in advance but swept. The weightings of \cite{dir} use a
smoothed histogram, for which a kernel density estimate was substituted here. DenseWeight
\cite{denseweight}, a related scheme, weights linearly in the density rather than by a power and was
not used.

\section{Decision rule}
\label{app:decision}

Four quantities were computed per compound and averaged: \emph{order}, the within-compound Spearman
correlation; \emph{top of order}, the share of the \CfgTopK{} most sensitive lines that the model also
ranks in its top \CfgTopK; \emph{values}, the MAE in the target's units; and \emph{spread}, the slope of
a regression of truth on prediction, which is 1 for a calibrated model, by analogy with the calibration
slope of risk prediction models \cite{vancalster2016}. A configuration counted as an improvement on the
default only if it was better on order and worse on no other quantity by more than that quantity's own
seed band, the half-range over the default's three seeds.
% (\code{notebooks/5\_evaluation.ipynb} cell 6, \code{decide()}: the band pooled the challenger's and the
% default's six seeds until 21.10.2026, while this text said three)
The default was the unweighted MAE configuration on PCA rather than unweighted MSE, because unweighted
MSE on PCA was the only configuration whose score differed between identical runs (Section~\ref{sec:results-q1}).

\section{Multiple-instance model and its evaluation}
\label{app:milcrit}

A bag was a complete cell line because, for a sub-bag of $B$ cells drawn from a line of $n$, the expected
loss is
\begin{equation}
  \mathrm{E}\,\mathcal{L}_B = \left(\bar p - y\right)^2 + \frac{\mathrm{Var}_m(p)}{B}\left(1 - \frac{B-1}{n-1}\right),
  \label{eq:subbag}
\end{equation}
with $\bar p$ and $\mathrm{Var}_m(p)$ the mean and variance of the predictions over all $n$ cells of the line
(Equation~\ref{eq:mil-decomposition}). This is the per-cell loss at $B = 1$ and contains no variance term
only at $B = n$. The bag size
therefore sets how strongly the objective charges for the quantity under study. Complete bags also make
each line one training example, removing the per-cell objective's weighting of lines by their cell count,
which ranges from \CellsMin{} to \CellsMax{}, at the cost of one gradient step per line.

A variance term in the loss was rejected
because it would make the spread stage pass by construction and change objective and architecture at
once. A maximum or quantile aggregator was rejected because it does not force variation any more than the mean does and does
not match an averaged label. This departs from Ilse et al., who, for binary labels under the assumption
that a bag is positive if any of its instances is, call the mean a poor pooling of instance scores
\cite{ilse2018}. Here the label is a continuous average over a line's cells, which the mean matches by
definition. Per-line centring of expression, rejected for the per-cell model (Section~\ref{sec:representations}), would
suit this question, but the MIL model used the same uncentred representations, so it remains untested.

The positive control was scored within the bag, on the per-cell predictions, because a score on the
bag's mean prediction is equally good for a model that gives all cells the same value.

In the input ceiling, the within-line share $s$ is the mean over lines, each weighted equally, of the within-line variance
divided by the total variance over the cells of all cross-validation lines, where variance is the trace
of the covariance. The ratio $(1-s)/s$ therefore does not depend on the units of an embedding, but each
dimension counts in proportion to its own variance, so for PCA the leading components dominate by
construction. This is the variance that a distance between cells sees. The interval for the difference
of the ratios between the representations came from a paired bootstrap over lines: lines were drawn
with replacement, each with all its cells, both representations were recomputed on the same resample,
and the 95\,\% percentile interval was taken over \BtwBootB{} resamples \cite{efron1993,lazic2010}.

As a description that gates nothing, the per-cell predictions were correlated within each line with the
eleven distinct program scores (Spearman). For the program with the largest association, the
correlation was recomputed with mitochondrial fraction controlled by Equation~\ref{eq:partial}, and the
program score was correlated within each line with sequencing depth, detected genes and mitochondrial
fraction.
